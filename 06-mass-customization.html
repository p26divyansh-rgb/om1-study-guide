Topic 6 of 12 · The four faces of mass customization: Collaborative, Adaptive, Cosmetic, Transparent.

Mass Production aims for efficiency through stability/control — standardized, low-cost, long product life cycles for large homogeneous markets. Mass Customization aims for variety & customization through flexibility and quick response — customized, high-quality, short product life cycles for fragmented/heterogeneous markets. Customization = product variety, so customers must be involved somewhere in the process.

The Four Faces of Mass Customization (Gilmore & Pine, HBR 1997)

TypeProduct change?Representation change?MeaningExample
CollaborativeYesYesCompany dialogues directly with the customer to design a bespoke productDell: choose your own RAM/CPU/storage; custom sneaker design
AdaptiveYes (by customer)NoStandard product, but customer can alter/configure it themselves after purchaseAdjustable office chair
CosmeticNoYesSame core product, different packaging/presentation per customerCoca-Cola bottles printed with different names
TransparentYesNo (customer not told explicitly)Company customizes based on known customer data, without askingHotel automatically gives a returning guest their preferred pillow
Exam trap: "Customer designs their own product on a webstore, company manufactures & ships it" → this is Collaborative customization (2025 exam Q8), not Adaptive — because the company (not the customer) does the actual physical customization, based on direct interaction/co-design with the customer.
