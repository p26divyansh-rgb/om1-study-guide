8 Supplier Relationships

Topic 8 of 12 · Traditional/adversarial vendor management vs. Toyota's Keiretsu partnership model.
Traditional/Adversarial Vendor Management
Multiple competing suppliers played off each other, negotiated purely on price, short-term contracts, low information sharing, suppliers "beaten up" on performance (e.g., pressuring a supplier to abandon a project).
Toyota's Lean / Keiretsu Model
Long-term partnerships, trust, mutual knowledge-sharing, suppliers treated as an extension of the firm. Toyota sets high standards and challenges suppliers, but also helps them improve — testing commitment with smaller orders before scaling up to a permanent relationship (from Toyota Way Principle 11).
Exam angle: identify whether a described buyer-supplier scenario reflects adversarial/traditional vendor management vs. a Toyota-style Keiretsu partnership, and state the trade-offs (short-term cost savings vs. long-term quality/innovation/resilience).
